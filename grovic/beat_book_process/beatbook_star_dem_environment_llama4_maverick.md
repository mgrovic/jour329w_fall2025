Core environmental issues on the Eastern Shore include water quality, development impacts, fisheries management, climate change, habitat restoration, and pollution control. Unresolved issues include lawsuits over aquaculture leases and policy debates on oyster restoration.

## 3) Aquaculture Deep Dive
Aquaculture on the Eastern Shore includes oyster, clam, mussel, and seaweed farming, with associated hatcheries and leasing programs. The Maryland Department of Natural Resources, Maryland Department of the Environment, U.S. Army Corps, local planning/zoning boards, and NOAA play key roles in regulating and supporting aquaculture. Friction points include siting conflicts, navigation concerns, habitat impacts, water quality issues, and working waterfront conflicts. Trends to watch include technological advancements, financing models, and legal challenges.

### Aquaculture Proposal Checklist
1. Location and lease details
2. Species being farmed
3. Regulatory permits and approvals
4. Environmental impact assessments
5. Local community response
6. Potential conflicts with other water users
